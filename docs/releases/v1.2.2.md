# Clipboard Manager v1.2.2

Select a text record and paste it directly back into the app you were using.
选中一条文字历史，直接粘贴回刚才使用的应用。

## Quick paste / 快捷粘贴

1. Focus the destination input, then press the configured global shortcut (default `Ctrl+Shift+V`). / 在目标输入框按全局热键，默认 `Ctrl+Shift+V`。
2. Search and use ↑/↓ or click a record to select it. The panel shows the destination app. / 搜索后用 ↑/↓ 或点击卡片选择记录，面板显示目标应用。
3. Press `Enter` or click **Paste selected**. The panel returns focus to the original window and sends Ctrl+V. / 按 `Enter` 或点击“粘贴选中记录”，恢复原窗口焦点并发送 Ctrl+V。
4. `Ctrl+Enter` copies without pasting; `Esc` cancels and hides the panel. / `Ctrl+Enter` 仅复制并收起，`Esc` 取消并收起。

Copy icons and Ctrl+1–9 retain their copy-only behavior. Tray opening retains the management workflow. Existing dialogs are preserved; close them before starting quick paste. Images remain copy-only. Sequential paste queues remain available for multiple records.

复制图标与 Ctrl+1–9 保持仅复制；托盘打开仍为管理模式。已有编辑弹窗会保留，请关闭弹窗后再使用快捷粘贴。图片暂时仅复制，多条记录仍可使用顺序粘贴队列。

## Reliability / 可靠性

- Each hotkey invocation binds one original window and process, and authorizes at most one paste. Switching away, cancelling and restarting discard this target.
- A short-lived native helper, launched by the foreground panel at confirmation time, restores the chosen window under normal Windows foreground rules. It checks the destination and clipboard sequence immediately before sending input and waits for Enter/modifiers to be released.
- The queue and quick paste share an input-operation lock. Failed or uncertain operations are not automatically retried. A usable copied text is retained when possible; check the destination before pasting manually to avoid duplicates. No Enter/Tab is sent to submit or advance a form.
- Elevated apps may block input, and apps can ignore Ctrl+V. Acceptance of native input is not proof that every third-party editor accepted the text. Focus refusal or a closed window falls back to a notice rather than another destination.

- 每次唤起绑定一个原窗口和进程，最多发送一次粘贴。切走、取消或重启后目标失效。
- 确认时由前台面板启动短时原生辅助进程，按 Windows 正常焦点规则恢复目标。发送前再次核对窗口和剪贴板序号，并等待 Enter 与修饰键释放。
- 快捷粘贴与顺序队列共用输入操作锁。失败或结果不确定时不自动重试；尽量保留已复制文字，提示先检查目标内容，避免重复粘贴。不发送用于提交或切换表单的 Enter/Tab。
- 管理员程序可能拦截输入，部分应用也会忽略 Ctrl+V。系统接受按键不等于所有编辑器都接受文字；焦点恢复被拒或窗口已关闭时提示处理，不向其他窗口输入。
